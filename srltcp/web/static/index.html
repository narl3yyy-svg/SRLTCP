Settings

JPEG, WebP and HEIC photos go out as JPEG with the longest side at most 2560 px, so they send faster over Tor or mobile data, without the location and camera details stored in the photo. Screenshots (PNG), GIFs and photos this device can't open are sent as they are. Off = the original file, untouched.

Your keys, contacts, chats and settings are stored encrypted on this device. On Android the key is kept by the phone's secure hardware (Keystore), so a copy of the app's files is useless anywhere else.

This phone Ask for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when SRLTCP is opened, and after 30 seconds away. Messages and calls still arrive while it's locked. A locked phone then shows only "SRLTCP — New message", and calls ring without the caller's name instead of opening over the lock screen. Once unlocked, notifications show who wrote and what.
Backup

One file with your identity, contacts, chats, groups and settings, locked with a password. Keep it safe: with the file and the password, someone could become you. Received files and shared folders are not included.

Most people never need to change anything here. SRLTCP picks the best path by itself: same Wi-Fi or VPN first, then a direct internet connection, then your relay, then Tor.

Over the internet Full speed between different networks (two mobile carriers, home Wi-Fi): the devices connect straight to each other over UDP, the way video calls do, finding each other through Tor or your relay. No port forwarding. Your public IP address is shared only with your contacts, inside the encrypted connection, and a STUN server (below) learns it too; on a direct connection your network can see which IP you talk to. Off = Tor or relay only: contacts never see your IP.
No server and no port forwarding needed: works from any Wi-Fi or mobile network. Used only when there's no faster path. Only your trusted contacts get your Tor address (inside the encrypted connection), and only they can connect through it; over Tor your contacts don't see your IP address. Both of you need it on. Tor is built into the Android app; on a PC install Tor Browser.
The relay only forwards sealed, padded packets: it can't read messages, see names or who you talk to, or fake anything. See the Networking guide in the docs for details.

Synced folders with contact


You both keep the same copy of a folder: changes on either side are copied to the other whenever you're connected, end-to-end encrypted. If you both change a file before it syncs, both versions are kept. Deleted files go to the other side's hidden .srltcp-sync/trash folder for 30 days.
