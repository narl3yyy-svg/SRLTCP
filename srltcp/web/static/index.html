Settings

Photos go out smaller (longest side 2560 px), so they send faster over Tor or mobile data. Shrinking also removes the location and camera details stored in the photo. Off = the original file, untouched.

Most people never need to change anything here. SRLTCP picks the best path by itself: same Wi-Fi or VPN first, then a direct internet connection, then your relay, then Tor.

Over the internet Connects straight through both routers, no port forwarding. Your public IP address is shared only with your contacts, inside the encrypted connection. Off = relay only: contacts never see your IP.
No server and no port forwarding needed: works from any Wi-Fi or mobile network. Used only when there's no faster path. Only your trusted contacts get your Tor address (inside the encrypted connection), and only they can connect through it; over Tor your contacts don't see your IP address. Both of you need it on. Tor is built into the Android app; on a PC install Tor Browser.
The relay only forwards sealed, padded packets: it can't read messages, see names or who you talk to, or fake anything. See the Networking guide in the docs for details.
